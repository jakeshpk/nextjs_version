## Why Monsoon Electrical Safety Matters

Kerala receives some of the heaviest rainfall in India, and with it comes a significant increase in electrical accidents. According to KSEB data, electrical incidents rise by nearly **60%** during the monsoon months (June–September).

As KSEB Class B Licensed Electrical Contractors with years of experience across Thrissur and Kerala, V4 Building Solutions has compiled the most critical safety measures you should take.

## Before the Monsoon Arrives

### 1. Get a Professional Electrical Inspection

The most important step you can take is getting your electrical system inspected by a licensed professional. Key areas to check:

- **Earthing system** — Ensure proper grounding is intact
- **Circuit breakers and MCBs** — Test all protective devices
- **Wiring insulation** — Check for any exposed or deteriorated wiring
- **External junction boxes** — Verify waterproof sealing

### 2. Install ELCB/RCCB Protection

An **Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker (ELCB)** or **Residual Current Circuit Breaker (RCCB)** can save lives. These devices detect current leakage and instantly disconnect the supply:

- Install 30mA RCCB for socket circuits
- Install 100mA RCCB for main incoming supply
- Test the trip mechanism monthly using the test button

### 3. Upgrade Outdoor Wiring

All outdoor electrical installations should be:

- **IP65 rated** or higher for water resistance
- Protected with **weatherproof enclosures**
- Installed with proper **drip loops** to prevent water tracking along cables
- Connected through **GI conduits** rather than exposed PVC

## During the Monsoon

### Emergency Dos and Don'ts

**DO:**
- Switch off the main supply if flooding occurs
- Use rubber footwear when handling any electrical equipment
- Keep emergency numbers handy (KSEB: 1912)
- Report fallen power lines immediately

**DON'T:**
- Never touch electrical equipment with wet hands
- Avoid using electrical appliances near water accumulation
- Don't attempt DIY repairs during rain
- Never use metal ladders near power lines

### Signs You Need Immediate Professional Help

Contact a licensed electrician immediately if you notice:

- Sparking from switches or outlets
- Burning smell from any electrical point
- Frequent MCB tripping
- Discoloration or heat around switchboards
- Tingling sensation when touching appliances

## Post-Monsoon Checklist

After the rains subside, conduct a thorough post-monsoon check:

1. **Test all earthing points** — Moisture can affect earth resistance
2. **Inspect panel boards** — Look for moisture ingress or corrosion
3. **Check UPS/inverter batteries** — Humidity can affect battery performance
4. **Review outdoor lighting** — Replace any water-damaged fixtures
5. **Document and repair** — Log any issues for insurance or future reference
